CGScout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: CGScout has no account and no server. Everything you enter (players, reports, photos, notes) is stored only on your device. We never receive, see or sell your data. Data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it.

Who we are

CGScout is developed by Christian Giallombardo (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at info@gics.lu.

Data stored on your device

CGScout lets you record scouting information, such as player names, dates of birth, nationalities, clubs, photos, match reports, ratings, notes and links, as well as your own scout profile and logo. All of it is saved in a local database on your device. We do not operate any server, and none of this data is sent to us or to any cloud service by the app.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.

Document scanning (Google ML Kit)

When you scan a printed player list, CGScout uses Google ML Kit to recognise the text. Recognition happens entirely on your device: your images and the recognised text are never sent to Google or to us.

To keep this feature working, ML Kit may send Google limited technical information: device model and operating-system version, app identifier and version, performance metrics, feature events and error codes, together with a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you. This data is used by Google for diagnostics and usage analytics and does not include your scouting data. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: only to take or choose a player photo, your logo, or a document to scan. Pictures stay on your device.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: only if you turn on the app lock. Your device checks your biometrics; CGScout never receives biometric data. Your unlock code is stored in the device’s secure storage and is never included in backups.
  • Notifications: only for backup reminders you schedule. They are created on your device; no push service is used.

Exports, sharing and backups

You can export backups, PDF reports and files. CGScout then opens your device’s share sheet, and you decide where the file goes (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, email or a messaging app). Once shared, the file is handled by that service under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust.

Like any app, CGScout’s data may be included in your device’s own system backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) if you have it turned on. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

Data about the players you scout

The information you record about players is personal data about other people, and some of them may be minors. You decide what you record and what you share, so you are responsible for handling it lawfully. If you use CGScout professionally, for example as a scout or club in the European Union, you act as the data controller under the GDPR. CGScout helps by keeping data on your device, offering an app lock, and letting you edit, export or delete records at any time.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a single player or report, erase all scouting data in Settings → Danger zone, or delete the app to remove everything stored by it. Copies you have exported or shared are not affected and must be deleted where you saved them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your data, we cannot access, correct or delete it for you; you have full control over it in the app. For the technical data collected by Google ML Kit, you can exercise your rights with Google. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

CGScout is a tool for scouts and coaches and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how CGScout handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app.
